## Why

The reviewed typed Kotlin/Swift boundary makes direct Kotlin Toolchain development viable without the iOS Gradle/SKIE bridge. The maintainer approved applying this bounded default after candidate interoperability, native and operational checks.

## What Changes

- Apply the reviewed explicit projections and direct iOS development/test/unsigned defaults, preserving native shells and current async behavior.
- **BREAKING**: Reject stale Gradle selector overrides and hold credentialed iOS delivery until separately authorized and validated.
- Record the approval, refresh applicable risk evidence and bind the applied source without extending the existing exception expiry.
- Validate the integrated source independently of historical candidate and hosted evidence.
- Preserve bridge files for complete content rollback; physical deletion remains a separate gate.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `direct-ios-default`: Direct development selection, typed interoperability, guarded delivery and reversible integration.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Shared DI projections, native iOS adapters and project integration, repo-owned development/CI scripts, Fastlane defaults, six workflow fallback declarations and public architecture documentation. Dependency pins, native feature semantics and bridge/catalog bytes remain unchanged. Credentialed delivery and physical deletion are excluded.
