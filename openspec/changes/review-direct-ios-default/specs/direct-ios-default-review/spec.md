## Purpose

Provide a concrete source-bound interop and caller review for a reversible direct iOS default while preserving current production and independent approval gates.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Typed boundary review preserves feature meaning
The review SHALL retain exhaustive mutually exclusive states, concrete payloads, typed reasons, explicit factories and native presentation ownership. It MUST document the source and evidence for cancellation/failure behavior and distinguish preservation from a redesigned async policy.

#### Scenario: Preserved current async behavior
- **WHEN** a builder migration is proposed
- **THEN** the review states existing error/cancellation fallbacks and measured limits without claiming automatic cross-language cancellation or changing those semantics implicitly

### Requirement: Proposed callers execute their reported builder
The review SHALL enumerate every local, CI, native-project and delivery caller and bind a coherent draft. In direct-content candidates an incompatible or unknown builder selector MUST refuse before framework compilation. Credentialed delivery MUST remain unavailable until separately authorized and evidenced.

#### Scenario: Stale retained-builder override
- **WHEN** a direct-content candidate receives a retained-builder override
- **THEN** it refuses with content rollback guidance rather than silently executing and reporting different builders

#### Scenario: Credentialed delivery attempted during unsigned scope
- **WHEN** a credentialed iOS archive/export/upload entry point is invoked on the scoped candidate
- **THEN** it refuses before signing or API-key processing and retains unsigned assessment capability

### Requirement: Draft identity and rollback remain independent of adoption
The review SHALL bind exact source, path allowlist, file bytes/modes and patch identity; preserve bridge files; validate application and reversal in isolated owned paths; and retain every unmeasured gate. It MUST NOT alter production defaults, risk scope/expiry or authorize physical deletion by completing this review.

#### Scenario: Exact reverse application
- **WHEN** the reviewed draft is reversed in an isolated source copy
- **THEN** all original source bytes and modes are restored and native execution evidence is distinguished from byte restoration

#### Scenario: Maintainer has approved only adoption and assessment
- **WHEN** the review package is complete under the existing authorization
- **THEN** the default/removal decisions remain separate and the current production selection remains retained
